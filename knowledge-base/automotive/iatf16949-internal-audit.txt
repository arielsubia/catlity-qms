IATF 16949 study note: internal audit

Study reference for the Catlity QMS normative agent.
This note is an original summary of topics an auditor reviews. It is not the official text of IATF 16949 and it does not reproduce the standard.

An automotive quality system is examined in more than one way. A system audit asks whether the management system meets its own requirements. A manufacturing-process audit asks whether the shop floor follows the control plan and the work instructions. A product audit asks whether the finished product meets the specified requirements. The auditor looks for all three in the programme, with a frequency that reflects risk and customer performance.

The programme is a schedule, not a wish list. Completed audits have dates, auditors, scope, and a record of what was seen. Processes that run on more than one shift are covered over time. A process with customer complaints or unstable indicators is audited sooner than a stable one. The auditor compares last year's complaints with the audit plan.

Auditors are competent for the type of audit they perform and independent of the activity. A process owner does not audit their own process. Competence can come from training and from experience; the record shows both the qualification and the audits the person has done.

Findings lead to containment when product may be affected, and to corrective action when the cause can recur. The auditor reads one process-audit finding through to the effectiveness check. Closing a finding because the instruction was rewritten, without looking at the station again, is not enough.

Customer-specific audit rules, when a customer defines a method or a checklist, are included in the programme. The organization can show which method was used on a given audit.
